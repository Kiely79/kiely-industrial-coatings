Container and skip repainting in Northamptonshire

K.I.C. helps scope repainting projects for storage containers, skips and site cabins for hire firms, storage sites and depots in Northamptonshire. Where suitable, we can coordinate an independent specialist subcontractor. Any per-unit price is confirmed in the written proposal.

On-site work may avoid moving units, subject to the appointed subcontractor's equipment, access and site assessment.

What you get

  • An initial review of unit types, quantities and photographs.
  • A proposed scope and price, subject to subcontractor availability.
  • Any site inspection, programme and handover arrangements agreed in writing.
  • Payment terms and the responsible contracting party identified in the proposal.
