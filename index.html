A family heritage in industrial finishing

KIC is built on Northamptonshire’s manufacturing history and dedicated to our father’s lifelong trade. Our family ran the county’s premier industrial wet paint and powder coating operation, with two continuous track lines, thermal curing ovens, heavy-duty box ovens and precision spray booths.

John Kiely grew up in his father’s industrial finishing business, working weekends from the age of 10 and full time from 15, until the family firm closed in 2008. He then spent years spraying farm buildings, sheds, steelwork and railings in Ireland. Today he carries that experience forward through KIC, pairing stove enamel and wet paint expertise with Graco airless systems and 2K chemical-cure technology.

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Northamptonshire and cover the whole UK.

How do I get a quotation?

Call or WhatsApp John Kiely, or use the form below. Send the site location, the surface type, the approximate area and any photographs. We will reply with a priced specification.

Which coating system do I need?

It depends on the surface and the environment. Tier 1 suits cosmetic refreshes, Tier 2 heavy anti-corrosive restoration, Tier 3 chemical and abrasion exposure, and Tier 4 floors and walkways. We recommend the right tier after reviewing your site.

Do you apply waterproof membranes?

No. We apply industrial paint coatings only.

How do you prepare the surface?

Preparation includes 3,000 PSI industrial washdown with degreasers and mechanical rust removal back to clean parent metal, before coating begins.

Are you insured, and do you provide RAMS?

Yes. We hold £10M Public and Products Liability insurance, access plant is hired with certified operators on every job, and a site-specific RAMS is issued before we mobilise.

What guarantee do you provide?

Every coating system we apply is guaranteed by its manufacturer. The guarantee is confirmed for each tier and each product used on your job, with the period stated on your quotation and certificate of completion.

What happens when the work is finished?

We carry out a 6-point walkthrough with your Site Agent, then issue a certificate of completion and an aftercare guide. The final balance is due on sign-off.
