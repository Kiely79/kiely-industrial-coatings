Fixed-price packages

Two simple ways to buy, and a thank-you for introductions. Paint coatings only, no membranes or building repairs.

Lease-End Pack

When a surveyor's schedule lists repainting, we do it as one fixed-price job: walls, ceilings, steelwork, cladding, rolled epoxy floors and floor lines. You get before and after photos with dates to send back to the surveyor. Out-of-hours work available.

Annual Care Plan

One fixed yearly price. Each year we wash the roof or cladding, check the paint, fasteners and gutters, and spray or roll touch-up paint where needed. You get a short photo report and a reminder when a full repaint is due.

Finder's fee

Know a building that needs coating? Introduce a new client to us and earn 3% of the job price (ex VAT) when the job is paid. Not open to anyone employed by or advising the client. Email us for the terms.

A family heritage in industrial finishing

KIC is built on Northamptonshire’s manufacturing history and dedicated to our father’s lifelong trade. The family ran specialist industrial wet paint and powder coating businesses, with continuous track lines, thermal curing ovens, heavy-duty box ovens and precision spray booths.

John Kiely grew up in the family business, working weekends from the age of ten and full time from fifteen, until the family firm closed in 2008. He then spent years spraying farm buildings, sheds, steelwork and railings. Today he carries that experience forward through KIC, pairing stove enamel and wet paint expertise with Graco airless equipment and two-pack chemical-cure coating systems.

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Northampton and cover the whole UK.

How do I get a quotation?

Call or WhatsApp John Kiely, or use the form below. Send the site location, the surface type, the approximate area and any photographs, and we will reply with a priced specification.

Which coating system do I need?

It depends on the surface and the environment. Tier 1 suits cosmetic refreshes, Tier 2 heavy anti-corrosive restoration, Tier 3 heavy wear and abrasion, and Tier 4 floors and walkways. We recommend the right tier after reviewing your site.

Do you apply waterproof membranes?

No. We apply industrial paint coatings only.

How do you prepare the surface?

Preparation starts with an industrial pressure washdown and degreasing, then mechanical rust removal back to clean parent metal, before any coating begins.

Do you provide RAMS and insurance details?

A site-specific RAMS is issued before we mobilise, and access plant is hired with operators where work at height requires it. Our insurance cover is confirmed in writing with your quotation.

Can you work out of hours?

Yes, by arrangement. Evenings and weekends suit shops, factories and depots that cannot stop trading.

What is the Lease-End Pack?

A fixed-price repaint of the items on a surveyor's schedule, with before and after photos. Paint coatings and rolled floors only.

Do you offer yearly upkeep?

Yes, the Annual Care Plan: a yearly wash, a check of the paint, fasteners and gutters, and touch-up paint, for a fixed price.

What warranty do you provide?

Warranty terms depend on the coating system and the condition of the surface. They are confirmed in writing on your quotation before any work starts.

What happens when the work is finished?

We carry out a six-point walkthrough with your site agent, then issue a certificate of completion and an aftercare guide. The final balance is due on sign-off.
