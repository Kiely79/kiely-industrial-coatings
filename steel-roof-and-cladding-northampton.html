Steel roof and cladding coating in Northampton

K.I.C. helps scope industrial paint-coating projects for profiled steel roofs, cladding, steelwork and gutters. Where suitable, we can coordinate an independent specialist subcontractor for owners and managers of industrial units, yards and farm buildings in and around Northampton.

A good coating protects the steel and tidies the building. It also helps with lease-end repaints and with planned yearly upkeep.

What you get

  • An initial review of the information and photographs you provide.
  • A written proposed scope and price, subject to subcontractor availability.
  • Any site inspection, start date and handover arrangements agreed in writing.
  • Payment terms and the responsible contracting party identified in the proposal.
