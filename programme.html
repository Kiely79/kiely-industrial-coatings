Many sites? One plan, one rate.

We repaint the outside of industrial buildings: roofs, steel cladding, steelwork and gutters. If you look after several sites, we can set up a 3-year programme. One or two sites each quarter, in the order you choose.

  • The rate is locked for the 3 years and written in the plan.
  • A written fixed price for every site before we start.
  • A free roof and cladding check on each site first.
  • One contact, one invoice, one diary.

Send us your site list and we will come back with a plan and a price.

Founding client offer

5% off jobs of £25,000 (ex VAT) and over, for our first 10 jobs booked before 31 March 2027. In return we ask for a short written reference or case study with photos. Naming your company is your choice. The offer is shown as a line on our written quote, and it ends when 10 jobs are booked, on 31 March 2027, or earlier if our diary is full. It does not combine with the multi-unit rate: you get whichever saving is bigger.

Multi-unit rate

If you book 6 to 10 units together on one site, estate or owner, we take 4% off the whole lot. For 11 or more units it is 8.5%. One set-up covers every unit, so the saving is real. Five units or fewer do not get a multi-unit discount.

How payment works

Every job has a written fixed price. Smaller jobs: 70% deposit and 30% at handover. Mid-size jobs: 50% deposit and 50% at handover. Larger jobs can be staged: 50% deposit, 30% about half way, 20% at handover. Each stage is shown in the quote and is due 7 days from the invoice. We order paint once the deposit has cleared, and changes are agreed in writing first.

All offers are subject to our written quote.
