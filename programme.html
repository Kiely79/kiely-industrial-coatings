Many sites? One plan, one rate.

K.I.C. can help scope a multi-site industrial coating programme and coordinate independent specialist subcontractors for roofs, steel cladding, steelwork and gutters. Any three-year programme, site sequence and delivery commitments are subject to contractor availability and a written agreement for each site.

  • Proposed pricing and any price-lock period are set out in the written agreement.
  • Each site's scope, subcontractor, price and programme are agreed before work is instructed.
  • Any site inspection is arranged and confirmed separately.
  • K.I.C. coordinates communication; invoicing and payment responsibilities are stated in the project agreement.

Send us your site list for an initial review. We will confirm whether the work can be scoped and whether a suitable independent subcontractor may be available.

Founding client offer

5% off jobs of £25,000 (ex VAT) and over, for our first 10 jobs booked before 31 March 2027. In return we ask for a short written reference or case study with photos. Naming your company is your choice. The offer is shown as a line on our written quote, and it ends when 10 jobs are booked, on 31 March 2027, or earlier if our diary is full. It does not combine with the multi-unit rate: you get whichever saving is bigger.

Multi-unit rate

If you book 6 to 10 units together on one site, estate or owner, we take 4% off the whole lot. For 11 or more units it is 8.5%. One set-up covers every unit, so the saving is real. Five units or fewer do not get a multi-unit discount.

How payment works

Payment terms, deposits, materials purchasing, invoicing and handover milestones depend on the agreed project structure and responsible contracting party. These are confirmed in writing before any work is instructed. K.I.C. does not commit a client or subcontractor to payment terms without agreement.

All offers are subject to our written quote.
